Our story

Roni built the oven before he built the walls

In 1987, Roni Marchetti bricked a wood-fired oven in a rented lot two years before the dining room around it existed — he wanted to get the fire right first. Nearly four decades later, that same oven (patched, loved, and still standing) bakes every pizza we serve. We're still family-run, still cold-fermenting our dough for 48 hours, and still closing the register by hand every night.

1987

Roni finishes the wood-fired oven by hand and opens a four-table dining room next to it.

1996

The Sunday-night crowd outgrows the dining room; Roni's daughter Lena joins to run the front of house.

2009

Roni's Supreme wins the city's "Best Local Pie" reader poll for the first of what would be six times.

2026

Now run by Lena and her son Theo, Roni's still fires every pizza in that same brick oven.
